01Background
  • Periodic occupational examinations generate large volumes of test results.
  • Whether artificial intelligence (AI) validly interprets them remains unclear.
Aim: to review the evidence on AI interpreting workers' examination tests against a clinical reference standard.

Limitations of the current evidence
Evidence centres on chest radiography (9 of 13 studies).
Clinical-setting spirometry and ECG AI performs well but is unvalidated in workers.2,3
Reference standards are imperfect: human readers agreed only moderately (kappa 0.42–0.66).1,4
One system was deployed in more than 10 hospitals without reported implementation outcomes.5
04Conclusion

Evidence is insufficient to support AI interpretation in periodic examinations. AI is best positioned as decision support under physician responsibility.

Adoption requires:
External validationPrevalence-specific NPVComparison with occupational physiciansProspective evaluation
