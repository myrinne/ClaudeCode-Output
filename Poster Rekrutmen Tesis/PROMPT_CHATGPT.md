# Prompt ChatGPT: ilustrasi hero poster rekrutmen tesis

Cara pakai: tempel satu prompt per percakapan baru di ChatGPT (mode gambar). Minta 2 varian bila perlu. Simpan hasilnya sebagai PNG, lalu kirim ke saya. Teks, nomor, dan logo saya tambahkan sendiri di layout, jadi **gambar tidak boleh memuat huruf apa pun**.

Gaya yang dikunci (dari preferensi poster Anda): semi-flat vector, tanpa garis tepi hitam, bentuk dibedakan oleh warna, bayangan gradasi lembut, matte (bukan 3D mengilap), tanpa lantai memantul.

---

## A. Termometer (portrait 1024 x 1536)

```
Create a vertical hero illustration for a health-research recruitment poster.

Subject: one large, centered, upright thermometer. Rounded glass tube with a round bulb at the bottom, filled with a warm orange gradient liquid (light peach #FFB089 at the top of the liquid to deep orange #E2582A at the bulb). The tube is white/very light with a thin dark teal (#0F3D47) rim and 8 small evenly spaced tick marks on each side. Behind the thermometer, a soft round warm glow (pale peach, fading to transparent). Two small groups of soft wavy heat lines rising on the left and right, in orange (#E2582A) at 70% opacity.

Style: semi-flat vector illustration, NO black outlines, shapes defined by colour, soft gradient shading so it feels slightly 3D, matte finish, clean and professional, minimal detail. No glossy reflections, no reflective floor, no drop shadow on a surface.

Background: plain solid warm off-white #F6F3EE, nothing else.
Palette limit: teal #0F3D47, orange #E2582A, peach #FFB089, off-white. 
Strictly NO text, NO numbers, NO letters, NO logos, NO people, NO labels anywhere in the image.
Leave about 15% empty margin around the thermometer.
```

## B. Irisan kulit lengan bawah (landscape 1536 x 1024)

```
Create a hero illustration for a health-research recruitment poster: an isometric cutaway block of human forearm skin, shown as a clean educational diagram.

Subject: one isometric rectangular block of skin cut open at the front. Layers from top to bottom: a thin light peach epidermis (#FFD7C2), a thicker pink-coral dermis (#F59FA0 to #E97F85), and a pale yellow subcutaneous fat layer (#FFE29A). Inside the pink dermis, a single horizontal red blood vessel (tube, #E5384A to #A8182F) with a few small white blood cells inside it. On the top surface, two small light-blue sweat droplets. Above the block, a small handheld skin-measurement probe (dark navy #14213D rounded body, one small mint-green dot, a short blue #3D5AFE tip) hovering just above the skin surface, pointing down. Above everything, five soft coral (#EF5B5B) wavy heat lines.

Style: semi-flat vector, NO black outlines, shapes defined by colour, soft gradient shading to feel isometric and 3D but matte, simplified and clean, professional medical-infographic look. No glossy reflections, no reflective floor, no photorealism.

Background: plain solid very light blue #EEF2FB, nothing else. Leave about 12% empty margin around the subject.
Strictly NO text, NO numbers, NO letters, NO labels, NO logos, NO people, NO hands anywhere in the image.
```

---

## Kalau hasilnya kurang pas, tambahkan satu baris ini

- Terlalu mengkilap: `Make it completely matte, flat-vector, like a Greenpeace campaign illustration. Remove all highlights and reflections.`
- Ada huruf/angka muncul: `Remove every letter and number. The image must contain zero text.`
- Ada garis hitam: `Remove all black outlines. Use colour fills only.`
- Latar tidak polos: `Make the background one flat solid colour #F6F3EE (or #EEF2FB for B) with no gradient, no vignette.`
