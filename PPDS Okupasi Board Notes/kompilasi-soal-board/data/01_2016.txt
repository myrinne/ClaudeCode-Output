### short | TOX | 2016
Q: How is smoking at the workplace connected to lead toxicity?
A: Smoking encourages hand-to-mouth behavior. Eating and drinking in the workplace have a similar effect.
### short | TOX | 2016
Q: Can lead (stored in bone) be mobilized back into circulation?
A: Yes, during stress, pregnancy, lactation, chronic disease, or possibly osteoporosis.

### short | ERGO | 2016
Q: Regarding biomechanics, the components that comprise a moment are ____ and ____.
A: Force (weight of the object) and distance (perpendicular distance from the axis of rotation).
### short | ERGO | 2016
Q: List 7 ergonomic risk factors.
A: Vibration, force, posture/position, repetition, duration, compression (contact stress), temperature.
### short | ERGO | 2016
Q: If an ergonomist knows the work intensity of a task, (s)he can recommend modifying the task. What objective measures can be used to estimate work intensity?
A: Respiratory rate; cardiac output (heart rate); metabolic rate (calorimetry, oxygen uptake); core body temperature.
### short | ERGO | 2016
Q: The neutral posture of a joint is determined by the ____ (minimum, maximum, resting) length of the muscle crossing that joint.
A: Resting.
### short | ERGO | 2016

### short | HI | 2016
Q: What are BEIs?
A: Biological Exposure Indices. They provide warning levels of biological response to a substance or agent.
### short | HI | 2016
Q: What does the BEI generally indicate?
A: A concentration below which nearly all workers should not experience adverse health effects.
### short | HI | 2016
Q: What does air monitoring against the TLV indicate?

Q: Regarding urine specimen acceptability for BEI testing, what guidelines are indicated for acceptable specimens?
A: Creatinine concentration > 0.3 g/L and < 3.0 g/L, or specific gravity > 1.010 and < 1.030.

### essay | PARU | 2016
Q: Define "occupational asthma" and "work-aggravated asthma".
A: Occupational asthma: variable airflow limitation and/or airway hyperresponsiveness due to causes and conditions attributable to a particular occupational environment, not to stimuli encountered outside the workplace. Work-aggravated asthma: pre-existing asthma aggravated by a workplace exposure.
