### short | HI | 2016-Remed
Q: An exposure to acetone at the following concentrations and durations: 1000 ppm for 3 hours, 500 ppm for 2 hours, 200 ppm for 3 hours. The worker would have an 8-hour TWA exposure of ____ ppm.
A: (3×1000 + 2×500 + 3×200) / 8 = (3000 + 1000 + 600) / 8 = 575 ppm.
### short | HI | 2016-Remed
Q: A worker is exposed to 3 chemicals whose effects are the same and additive. Chemical A: C = 500 ppm, L = 750 ppm. Chemical B: C = 100 ppm, L = 500 ppm. Chemical C: C = 50 ppm, L = 200 ppm (C = concentration, L = exposure limit). Determine whether the exposure combination is within the acceptable limit.
A: Em = 500/750 + 100/500 + 50/200 = 0.67 + 0.20 + 0.25 = 1.12. Em > 1, so the combined exposure exceeds the acceptable limit.

### short | BIO | 2016-Remed
Q: Is the common cold usually considered an occupationally related infectious disease? Why or why not?
A: No. Occupationally related infections are those with higher rates in a specific occupational group than in the community. Common cold rates do not differ substantially between work and general environments, so it is not normally considered occupational even though its incidence at work is appreciable.
