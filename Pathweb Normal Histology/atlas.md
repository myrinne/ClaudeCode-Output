# Normal Histology Atlas (condensed)

Own-words study notes built from viewing every annotated image of the NUS Pathweb Normal Histology section (55 organs, accessed 2026-10-20). Source: https://medicine.nus.edu.sg/pathweb/normal-histology/ . Images and page text are kept locally in `raw/` (not in git, NUS copyright).

How to use: for each organ, **ID clues** = what lets you name the organ at low power; **Pathweb labels** = structures annotated on the reference images; **Look-alikes** = what it gets confused with and the separating feature; **Images** = `raw/images/<organ>/` (`*-blank` = unlabelled quiz version).

---

## Cardiovascular

### Adrenal gland
- ID clues: thin, often Y/V-shaped gland in fat; yellow-pink cortex wrapping a central purple-grey medulla.
- Pathweb labels: capsule; cortex with zona glomerulosa (thin, small clustered cells under capsule), zona fasciculata (broad zone of pale, lipid-vacuolated cells in radial cords), zona reticularis (compact eosinophilic cells in anastomosing cords, can carry lipofuscin); medulla (basophilic-amphophilic chromaffin cells in nests, granular cytoplasm).
- Look-alikes: clear-cell RCC / renal cortex (adrenal has zonation, no tubules/glomeruli); liver (no portal tracts, cells in radial cords not plates around central veins).

### Atrioventricular valve and chordae tendineae
- ID clues: thin, paucicellular, pale leaflet floating free; round-oval dense pink collagen cords (chordae) nearby.
- Pathweb labels: endothelium on both surfaces; fibrosa (dense collagen core, ventricular side), spongiosa (loose, proteoglycan-rich, bluish); chordae = collagen covered by endothelium.
- Look-alikes: tendon (no endothelial covering, parallel fibroblasts); myxomatous valve (spongiosa expansion, disrupted fibrosa).

## Gastrointestinal / hepatobiliary

### Ampulla of Vater
- ID clues: duodenal mucosa with villi meeting a protruding papilla; underlying channel with complex glands, smooth-muscle bundles, and pancreatic lobules deep.
- Pathweb labels: major papilla, duodenal mucosa and lumen, distal CBD / lumen of ampulla, ampulla, sphincter of Oddi (smooth muscle bundles), peri-biliary glands, pancreatic parenchyma; epithelium = intestinal type (with goblet cells) on the duodenal surface, transitioning to pancreatobiliary type (simple columnar, no/rare goblet cells) inside.
- Look-alikes: adenocarcinoma infiltrating the ampulla (normal peri-biliary glands stay lobular, bland, and do not sit naked in stroma or perineural); duodenum alone (no muscular sphincter/biliary channel).

### Appendix
- ID clues: small round tube, crypts without villi, mucosa/submucosa crowded with lymphoid follicles forming an almost continuous ring.
- Pathweb labels: mucosa, reactive lymphoid follicles, submucosa, muscularis propria, subserosa, serosa.
- Look-alikes: colon (larger lumen, lymphoid tissue patchy, not circumferential); ileum (villi, Peyer patches on one side).

### Bile duct (extrahepatic)
- ID clues: single duct lumen in fat with a thick wall of dense collagen (not smooth muscle), lined by one layer of tall columnar cells.
- Pathweb labels: lumen, wall (collagenous connective tissue), simple columnar epithelium, sacculi of Beale (epithelial outpouchings/pits), peribiliary glands (small lobular clusters in the wall), nerve bundles.
- Look-alikes: gallbladder (has muscularis, Rokitansky-Aschoff sinuses, no submucosa); well-differentiated cholangiocarcinoma (normal peribiliary glands are lobular, near the lumen, bland nuclei, not perineural).

## Urinary

### Urinary bladder
- ID clues: urothelium over loose lamina propria, then thick, coarse interlacing bundles of detrusor smooth muscle.
- Pathweb labels: mucosa, urothelium (transitional epithelium) with umbrella cells (large, sometimes binucleate surface cells), lamina propria, thin wispy muscularis mucosae, detrusor (muscularis propria).
- Look-alikes: ureter (small stellate lumen, thinner muscle coat); prostatic urethra. Pitfall: wispy muscularis mucosae vs detrusor bundles matters for staging (T1 vs T2).

## Blood vessels (aorta, coronary artery, coronary vein)
- ID clues: aorta = huge lumen, thick media of many wavy elastic lamellae alternating with smooth muscle; muscular artery = round lumen, thick media mostly smooth muscle, clear internal elastic lamina; vein = thin media, thick collagenous adventitia, flattened/oval lumen, may have valves.
- Pathweb labels: tunica intima (endothelium + thin subendothelial layer), tunica media (smooth muscle + elastic fibres), tunica adventitia (loose connective tissue with vasa vasorum and nerves); coronary artery shown atherosclerotic in epicardial fat; vein valve leaflet = fibroelastic intimal fold lined by endothelium (veins >2 mm).
- Special stain: Miller elastic van Gieson (EVG) shows elastic lamellae dark blue-black and wavy.
- Look-alikes: elastic vs muscular artery (count elastic lamellae, EVG); vein vs lymphatic (lymphatic has no muscle wall, no RBC).

## Musculoskeletal

### Bone (compact, cancellous, growth plate)
- ID clues: eosinophilic lamellar matrix with osteocytes in lacunae. Compact bone = dense, with Haversian canals in concentric lamellae (osteons). Cancellous = interconnecting trabeculae with marrow (red or yellow/fatty) between them.
- Pathweb labels: periosteum, compact bone, Haversian canal with neurovascular bundle, concentric lamellae, lacuna, osteocyte with dendritic processes (canaliculi), endosteum, trabeculae, parallel lamellae, yellow marrow (adipocytes), red/haematopoietic marrow, articular cartilage over subchondral cancellous bone.
- Growth plate zones (epiphysis → metaphysis): resting → proliferative (stacked columns) → hypertrophic (large vacuolated chondrocytes) → calcified cartilage → ossification zone (bone laid on cartilage cores, marrow).
- Look-alikes: woven bone (haphazard collagen, plump crowded osteocytes = fracture callus, Paget, tumour bone); dense collagen/hyalinised stroma (no lacunae/osteocytes).

### Bone marrow
- ID clues: bony trabeculae with a mix of fat cells and haematopoietic cells between them; scattered giant cells (megakaryocytes).
- Pathweb labels: bone trabecula, haematopoietic (red) marrow, granulocyte precursors (pale cytoplasm, irregular/indented nuclei), erythroid precursors (round dark "ink-dot" nuclei in clusters), megakaryocytes (huge, multilobated nucleus), RBCs.
- Look-alikes: cellularity estimate ≈ 100 − age (%); metastatic carcinoma clusters (cohesive, foreign epithelium); serous fat atrophy (gelatinous pink material).

### Breast
- ID clues: terminal duct-lobular units (TDLUs) as discrete clusters of small acini in loose intralobular stroma, set in dense pink interlobular fibrous stroma and fat.
- Pathweb labels: duct, TDLU, acinus, intralobular stroma (loose, cellular), interlobular stroma (dense), double-layered epithelium = inner luminal epithelial cell + outer myoepithelial cell.
- Key teaching point: two-cell layer (luminal + myoepithelial) = benign; lost in invasive carcinoma (confirm with p63 / SMMHC / calponin IHC).
- Look-alikes: salivary gland (serous acini with zymogen granules, striated ducts); sweat glands in skin; gynaecomastia (ducts only, no lobules).

## Respiratory

### Bronchus
- ID clues: airway with plates of hyaline cartilage, seromucinous glands in the submucosa, pseudostratified ciliated epithelium with goblet cells.
- Pathweb labels: bronchial mucosa, cilia, goblet cell, peribronchial (seromucinous) glands, serous cells (darker, pyramidal, granular) in demilunes, mucous cells (pale, foamy) in tubules, hyaline cartilage with chondrocytes in lacunae.
- Look-alikes: trachea (C-shaped cartilage ring, trachealis muscle posteriorly); bronchiole (no cartilage, no glands, simple ciliated columnar → cuboidal, club cells). Occ-med link: goblet cell hyperplasia + gland enlargement (Reid index > 0.4) = chronic bronchitis.

## Nervous system

### Cerebellum
- ID clues: tightly folded folia ("tree of life"); each folium = pale molecular layer outside, dense blue granular layer inside, white matter core (medulla).
- Pathweb labels: cortex (molecular layer, Purkinje cell layer, granular layer), cerebellar medulla (white matter); Purkinje neurons = large flask-shaped cells in a single row at the molecular-granular junction with Nissl substance.
- Look-alikes: granular layer vs lymphocytes/small blue cell tumour (granule cells are uniform, sit in a defined layer with "glomeruli" pale islands); cerebrum (no granular layer, 6 layers). Toxicology: Purkinje cell loss with alcohol, mercury (methylmercury → granule cell loss), lithium/phenytoin.

### Cerebrum
- ID clues: pink fibrillary neuropil with scattered cells; cortex (grey matter) has more neuronal bodies, white matter is denser with rows of small round oligodendrocyte nuclei; pia covers the surface and dips into sulci.
- Pathweb labels: pia mater, sulcus, cortex/grey matter, white matter, neuropil, blood vessel; neuron (large triangular/pyramidal body, large pale nucleus, prominent nucleolus), oligodendrocyte (small round dark nucleus with clear perinuclear halo), astrocyte (paler, oval-elongated nucleus, scant visible cytoplasm), microglia (small rod/comma-shaped nucleus), myelinated axons.
- Look-alikes: oligodendroglioma ("fried egg" halo cells, but sheets/crowding); hippocampus (pyramidal band curled into C-shape); cerebellum (granular layer). Toxicology: red (hypoxic/ischaemic) neurons = shrunken, hypereosinophilic cytoplasm, pyknotic nucleus (CO poisoning: globus pallidus).

### Colon
- ID clues: flat mucosa, **no villi**, straight test-tube crypts packed with goblet cells, lymphoid follicles in mucosa/submucosa, two-layered muscularis propria.
- Pathweb labels: lumen, mucosa, crypts, goblet cells, lamina propria, muscularis mucosae, submucosa (vessels), lymphoid follicle, muscularis propria (inner circular + outer longitudinal) with Auerbach (myenteric) plexus ganglion cells between them, subserosa.
- Look-alikes: small bowel (villi, Paneth cells); appendix (lymphoid ring); rectum (similar; anorectal junction). Pitfall: ganglion cells absent in Hirschsprung disease (check submucosal and myenteric plexus).

## Eye

### Conjunctiva
- ID clues: thin stratified epithelium (2–5 layers of cuboidal to columnar cells) with interspersed goblet cells over loose vascular connective tissue (substantia propria).
- Pathweb labels: epithelium, goblet cells (common in inferior/nasal bulbar and forniceal conjunctiva), substantia propria / loose connective tissue, lymphatic channels, blood vessels; may contain lymphoid follicles and accessory lacrimal glands.
- Look-alikes: cornea (no goblet cells, avascular, regular stroma); oral mucosa/skin (keratinising or much thicker, no goblet cells); squamous metaplasia in dry eye (goblet cell loss, keratinisation).

### Cornea
- ID clues: avascular, very regular parallel collagen lamellae (with artefactual clefts) and sparse keratocytes; thin non-keratinised squamous epithelium on top, single-cell endothelium below.
- Pathweb labels: 6 layers outside → in: epithelium (stratified non-keratinising squamous, 5–7 layers), basement membrane (thin, PAS+ magenta), Bowman layer (acellular, below basement membrane), stroma (~90% thickness), Descemet membrane (thick PAS+ basement membrane of endothelium), endothelium (single flat layer) facing anterior chamber.
- Look-alikes: sclera (white, irregular collagen, vessels, opaque); conjunctiva (goblet cells, vessels). Pitfalls: Bowman layer lost in keratoconus; endothelial loss in Fuchs dystrophy (Descemet guttae).
- Extra (deep cornea): stroma ≈ 90% thickness, regularly spaced uniform collagen in proteoglycan matrix; clefts are processing artefact (normal); no vessels or lymphatics. Endothelium = neural-crest monolayer, declines with age, osmotic pump keeping stroma clear.

### Iris and ciliary body (anterior uvea)
- ID clues: heavily pigmented (brown-black) tissue behind the cornea; iris = thin diaphragm with pigmented posterior epithelium and loose stroma, **no anterior epithelium**; ciliary body = triangular muscular wedge with finger-like epithelium-covered folds.
- Pathweb labels: cornea, sclera, iris, iris stroma (melanocytes, vessels, nerves, loose CT; colour = number of stromal melanocytes), posterior epithelial lining, sphincter pupillae (circular smooth muscle near pupil margin); ciliary body: pars plicata (ciliary processes) anteriorly, pars plana posteriorly (→ ora serrata), ciliary smooth muscle (accommodation), stroma, two-layer epithelium = inner non-pigmented (→ continues as neurosensory retina) + outer pigmented (→ continues as RPE); trabecular meshwork (aqueous outflow to Schlemm canal).
- Look-alikes: choroid (flat, vascular, under retina); uveal melanoma (expansile mass of spindle/epithelioid cells, not bilayered epithelium).

### Optic nerve
- ID clues: round CNS tract wrapped in meninges (pia/arachnoid/dura), cut into polygonal fascicles by fibrovascular septa; longitudinal view shows rows of glial nuclei between fibre bundles.
- Pathweb labels: optic disc, lamina cribrosa (sieve-like scleral plate where fibres become myelinated), central retinal artery and vein (in the nerve for ~8–15 mm behind globe), retina, sclera, pia + arachnoid, dura, nerve fibre bundles, fibrovascular septa, glial cells.
- Look-alikes: peripheral nerve (perineurium/epineurium, Schwann cells, no meninges); spinal cord (grey-matter H). Toxicology: methanol → optic nerve/retinal ganglion cell damage, demyelination behind lamina cribrosa.

### Retina, choroid and sclera
- ID clues: sclera = thick, dense, haphazard pink collagen bundles (opaque, few fibroblasts); choroid = loose, highly vascular, packed with pigmented melanocytes; retina = layered blue nuclear bands (two thick nuclear layers + ganglion cells).
- Pathweb labels: sclera (~5/6 of globe surface, cornea periphery → optic nerve), fibroadipose tissue outside, choroid (vessels, pigmented melanocytes, Bruch membrane against RPE), retinal pigment epithelium (monolayer, phagocytic, outermost retina); artefactual separation of retina and of choroid from sclera is common.
- Retina 10 layers (outer → inner): 1 RPE, 2 photoreceptors (rods/cones), 3 external limiting membrane, 4 outer nuclear layer, 5 outer plexiform, 6 inner nuclear layer, 7 inner plexiform, 8 ganglion cell layer (>1 cell thick only at macula), 9 nerve fibre layer, 10 inner limiting membrane.
- Look-alikes: cerebellar cortex (granular layer looks like nuclear layers, but no photoreceptors/RPE); retinoblastoma (sheets of small blue cells, Flexner-Wintersteiner rosettes). Pitfall: artefactual retinal detachment has no subretinal fluid/RPE changes; true detachment shows photoreceptor degeneration.

### Eye, whole globe
- Orientation map: cornea → anterior chamber (aqueous) → iris (pupil) → posterior chamber → lens (dense pink, homogeneous, often shatters) → vitreous body → retina (often artefactually detached) on choroid → sclera; optic nerve exits postero-nasally. Ciliary body anchors the lens via zonules.

## Female genital tract

### Fallopian tube
- ID clues: round muscular tube with a highly branched, "lace-like" plicae pattern of mucosal folds filling the lumen.
- Pathweb labels: lumen, mucosal folds, inner circular and outer longitudinal smooth muscle, epithelium = ciliated columnar cells + secretory peg cells (non-ciliated, dark nuclei).
- Look-alikes: vas deferens (very thick 3-layer muscle, small lumen, pseudostratified with stereocilia); endometrium; serous papillary tumour (complex branching but cytologic atypia). Teaching: fimbrial end = proposed origin of high-grade serous carcinoma (STIC).

### Gallbladder and cystic duct
- ID clues: tall simple columnar epithelium thrown into folds over lamina propria, then a thin, loose, discontinuous smooth-muscle coat, then thick perimuscular connective tissue/fat. **No muscularis mucosae and no submucosa.**
- Pathweb labels: lumen, mucosa (mucosal folds, columnar epithelium with apical vacuoles, occasional narrow eosinophilic "pencil cells", lamina propria), muscularis (loose bundles of circular/longitudinal/oblique fibres), adventitia (liver side) / subserosa with artery, vein, adipose tissue, mesothelium (serosal surface, free side). Mucus glands in the neck. Cystic duct: ~3 cm, highly pleated mucosa (spiral valves of Heister), mucosa / muscularis / subserosa, groups of mucus glands in the wall.
- Look-alikes: bile duct (collagenous wall, no muscle coat); small bowel/stomach (have muscularis mucosae and submucosa). Pitfall: Rokitansky-Aschoff sinuses (mucosal herniation into muscle) are not invasion; staging depth is easy to overcall because the wall has no submucosa.

### Heart (ventricular wall)
- ID clues: branching striated muscle fibres with **one or two central nuclei**, intercalated discs, perinuclear lipofuscin, rich capillary bed.
- Pathweb labels: endocardium (endothelium, dense connective tissue + smooth muscle, subendocardial layer with Purkinje fibres), myocardium (striations, intercalated discs, central nucleus, lipofuscin), epicardium/visceral pericardium (mesothelium, loose connective tissue, adipose tissue, autonomic nerve bundles, coronary vessels).
- Look-alikes: skeletal muscle (peripheral multiple nuclei, no branching, no intercalated discs); smooth muscle (spindle nuclei, no striations). Pitfall: epicardial fat infiltrating RV myocardium is normal with age, but fibro-fatty replacement suggests ARVC. Ischaemia timeline: wavy fibres / contraction bands → coagulative necrosis + neutrophils (1–3 d) → macrophages (3–7 d) → granulation tissue → scar.

### Hippocampus
- ID clues: medial temporal brain tissue with a C-shaped (seahorse) dark band of pyramidal neurons (cornu Ammonis) interlocking with a tighter, V/C-shaped band of small granule cells (dentate gyrus).
- Pathweb labels: cornu Ammonis subfields CA1 (next to subiculum), CA2, CA3, CA4 (hilus, inside the dentate); subiculum; dentate gyrus layers = polymorphic layer (hilus, "mossy" cells), granule cell layer, molecular layer; pyramidal cells.
- Look-alikes: cerebral cortex (layered sheet, no interlocking C-bands); cerebellum. Pathology hooks: CA1 (Sommer sector) is most hypoxia-sensitive (also CO poisoning, cardiac arrest); hippocampal sclerosis in temporal lobe epilepsy; neurofibrillary tangles early in Alzheimer disease.

### Kidney
- ID clues: cortex with round glomeruli (renal corpuscles) among convoluted tubules; medulla with straight tubules and collecting ducts, no glomeruli; medullary rays project into cortex.
- Pathweb labels: cortex, medulla, medullary rays (straight proximal/distal tubules + collecting ducts), renal pelvis; interlobar → arcuate (at corticomedullary junction) → interlobular arteries; renal corpuscle = glomerulus (capillary tuft + mesangium) + Bowman space + Bowman capsule; proximal convoluted tubule (simple cuboidal, eosinophilic, brush border/microvilli, lumen fuzzy), distal convoluted tubule (simple cuboidal, paler, cleaner lumen); medulla: thick ascending loop of Henle, collecting duct (cuboidal-columnar, clear cell borders).
- Look-alikes: thyroid (follicles with colloid, no glomeruli); adrenal; clear cell RCC. Occ-med/tox hooks: heavy metals (Cd, Pb, Hg) and solvents hit the proximal tubule first; lead → acid-fast intranuclear inclusions in PCT.

### Liver
- ID clues: sheets of large polygonal pink hepatocytes in one-cell-thick plates separated by sinusoids, with regularly spaced portal tracts and central veins.
- Pathweb labels: lobule, central vein (terminal hepatic venule), portal tract (portal venule = largest thin-walled, hepatic arteriole, bile ductule/duct lined by cuboidal cells), sinusoids, hepatocytes.
- Zonation: zone 1 periportal (oxygen-rich), zone 3 centrilobular (most susceptible to hypoxia and to toxins activated by CYP2E1 such as paracetamol, CCl4, halothane, ethanol).
- Look-alikes: adrenal cortex (no portal tracts); hepatocellular adenoma/well-differentiated HCC (loss of portal tracts, plates >3 cells thick, reticulin loss).

## Haematolymphoid

### Lymph node
- ID clues: encapsulated bean-shaped lymphoid organ in fat; subcapsular sinus under capsule; cortical follicles, paracortex, medulla with cords and sinuses.
- Pathweb labels: capsule, subcapsular sinus, cortex with lymphoid follicles (B cells) showing germinal centre (pale, mixed centroblasts/centrocytes, tingible body macrophages) and mantle zone (dark small lymphocytes), paracortex (T cells, high endothelial venules), medulla (medullary cords with plasma cells, sinuses with macrophages).
- Look-alikes: tonsil (surface squamous epithelium + crypts); spleen (no sinus/capsule pattern, white pulp around arterioles); thymus (lobules, Hassall corpuscles). Pathology: follicular lymphoma = back-to-back follicles, no polarity, no tingible bodies; silicotic nodules / anthracotic pigment in hilar nodes of exposed workers.

### Medulla oblongata
- ID clues: brainstem cross-section with a dorsal V-shaped notch (4th ventricle, open medulla) and a ventral, wavy "crumpled bag" grey band (inferior olivary nucleus) beside triangular pyramids.
- Pathweb labels (dorsal → ventral): fourth ventricle with ependymal lining, hypoglossal nucleus (clusters of large motor neurons near midline/lateral to ventricle floor), medial lemniscus (midline, decussating myelinated axon bundles, oligodendrocytes), inferior olivary nucleus (convoluted) with accessory nuclei, pyramids (corticospinal tracts).
- Look-alikes: pons (bulky transverse pontine fibres + pontine nuclei ventrally); midbrain (aqueduct, substantia nigra); spinal cord (H-shaped grey matter, central canal).

### Midbrain
- ID clues: brainstem section with a small central cerebral aqueduct dorsally, two ventral "ear-like" cerebral peduncles separated by the interpeduncular fossa, and a band of **brown-pigmented neurons** (substantia nigra).
- Pathweb labels: tectum (dorsal), tegmentum, substantia nigra, crus cerebri; cerebral peduncle = tegmentum + substantia nigra + crus; cerebral aqueduct; interpeduncular fossa; pigmented neurons with neuromelanin granules (accumulate with age).
- Look-alikes: locus coeruleus (pons, also pigmented, near 4th ventricle). Pathology: pallor/loss of nigral neurons + Lewy bodies = Parkinson disease; manganese parkinsonism spares nigra more and hits globus pallidus; MPTP destroys nigral neurons.

### Oesophagus
- ID clues: thick non-keratinising stratified squamous epithelium with papillae, muscularis mucosae, submucosa (with glands in places), two-layered muscularis propria, **adventitia** (no serosa for most of its length).
- Pathweb labels: mucosa (epithelium, lamina propria, muscularis mucosae), submucosa, muscularis propria (inner circular, outer longitudinal; skeletal upper third → mixed → smooth lower third), myenteric (Auerbach) plexus with ganglion cells, adventitia (loose connective tissue).
- Look-alikes: skin (keratin, adnexa, dermis); ectocervix/vagina (glycogenated squamous, no muscularis mucosae, no submucosal glands); tongue (skeletal muscle core, papillae). Pathology: Barrett = columnar metaplasia with goblet cells above GEJ.
- Extra: submucosal mucous glands drain via ducts to the surface (useful clue vs vagina/cervix).

### Lacrimal gland (orbit)
- ID clues: lobulated **purely serous** gland with interspersed fat, in orbital connective tissue; ducts of increasing calibre between lobules.
- Pathweb labels: lobules separated by loose connective tissue, acini (cuboidal-columnar serous cells, round nuclei, abundant basophilic secretory granules), ducts (columnar/cuboidal lining with flattened myoepithelial cells around them).
- Look-alikes: parotid (also serous, but has prominent striated ducts and more fat with age); pancreas (islets, no myoepithelium, centroacinar cells); breast. Pathology: IgG4-related dacryoadenitis, lymphoma (MALT), pleomorphic adenoma.

### Ovary
- ID clues: densely cellular "storiform" spindle-cell stroma (cortex) containing follicles at various stages and pale hyaline scars (corpora albicantia); vascular medulla.
- Pathweb labels: follicles, Graafian (antral) follicle with antrum, granulosa cells, theca cells, cumulus oophorus, corona radiata, zona pellucida, secondary oocyte; corpus albicans (hyalinised scar of old corpus luteum); ovarian stroma.
- Look-alikes: fibroma/thecoma (stroma without follicles); endometrial stroma. Pathology: corpus luteum = yellow festooned layer of large luteinised granulosa cells.

### Pancreas
- ID clues: lobulated, intensely staining gland: acini with basophilic bases and pink apical zymogen granules; scattered **pale round islets of Langerhans**; ducts in septa.
- Pathweb labels: lobules, acini, islets of Langerhans (paler, fine granular cytoplasm; source of neuroendocrine tumours), duct hierarchy acinus → intercalated duct (pale low cells, centroacinar start) → intralobular duct → interlobular duct (in septa) → main pancreatic duct of Wirsung (columnar lining, fibrous cuff).
- Look-alikes: parotid (no islets, striated ducts, fat, myoepithelium); lacrimal gland; acinar cell carcinoma. Pitfall: islet aggregation in chronic pancreatitis mimics NET or invasion.

### Parathyroid
- ID clues: small gland of sheets/cords of small uniform cells with round central nuclei and clear-to-pale cytoplasm, mixed with fat that increases with age.
- Pathweb labels: parathyroid tissue, adipose tissue/adipocytes, chief (principal) cells (majority, round nuclei, pale cytoplasm), oxyphil cells (larger, abundant granular eosinophilic cytoplasm, oncocyte-like, more in older people); blue rim = surgical margin ink.
- Look-alikes: thyroid (follicles with colloid; parathyroid can form follicles but has no birefringent calcium oxalate in colloid); adenoma (stromal fat lost, rim of normal compressed gland); lymph node.

### Pituitary (anterior)
- ID clues: nests and cords of mixed cells with three tinctorial types between a rich capillary network; no follicles, no glands.
- Pathweb labels: acidophils (pink cytoplasm: GH, prolactin), basophils (dark purple: ACTH, TSH, FSH/LH), chromophobes (pale, few granules), capillaries.
- Look-alikes: pituitary adenoma (monotonous single cell type, loss of nested acinar pattern on reticulin stain); parathyroid; paraganglioma.

### Pituitary (whole gland, pars intermedia, posterior pituitary, stalk)
- ID clues: two halves at low power: dark, cellular adenohypophysis vs pale, fibrillary neurohypophysis; between them a row of colloid-filled cysts (pars intermedia).
- Pathweb labels: adenohypophysis, pars intermedia with Rathke cleft remnants (colloid cysts lined by columnar/cuboidal epithelium, resemble thyroid follicles), neurohypophysis (posterior pituitary) = unmyelinated axons (fibrillary pink), pituicytes (glial cells with elongated nuclei), Herring bodies (axonal dilatations full of neurosecretory granules), capillaries; pituitary stalk (infundibulum) looks like posterior pituitary and joins the hypothalamus.
- Look-alikes: thyroid (pars intermedia cysts); schwannoma / pilocytic astrocytoma (posterior lobe is fibrillary spindle tissue); Rathke cleft cyst / craniopharyngioma.

### Pons
- ID clues: large rounded brainstem slab; bulky ventral (basal) pons with criss-crossing longitudinal and transverse fibre bundles separated by islands of pontine nuclei; dorsal tegmentum under the 4th ventricle.
- Pathweb labels: basal pons (longitudinal corticospinal fibres, transverse pontocerebellar fibres, pontine nuclei/neuronal cell bodies), fourth ventricle with ependymal lining, locus coeruleus (pigmented neuromelanin neurons in the lateral floor of the 4th ventricle, main noradrenaline source).
- Look-alikes: medulla (olive, pyramids); midbrain (substantia nigra, aqueduct). Pathology: central pontine myelinolysis (rapid Na+ correction) = demyelination in basal pons.

## Male genital tract

### Prostate
- ID clues: tubuloacinar glands with undulating, papillary-infolded luminal borders in a **fibromuscular (smooth-muscle-rich) stroma**; pink laminated concretions (corpora amylacea) in lumina.
- Pathweb labels: prostatic glands, fibromuscular stroma, corpora amylacea (more with age), two-cell lining = luminal secretory cell (pale, columnar) + basal cell (flattened, dark nucleus); prostatic urethra lined by urothelium.
- Look-alikes: adenocarcinoma (small crowded glands, **no basal cells** (p63/HMWCK negative, AMACR+), straight luminal border, prominent nucleoli, crystalloids, blue mucin); seminal vesicle (lipofuscin, bizarre nuclei, exuberant folds); BPH (nodules of glands/stroma).

## Head and neck / salivary

### Salivary gland
- ID clues: lobules of acini with intervening fat and septa; serous acini = dark purple pyramidal cells packed with zymogen granules; mucous tubules = pale, foamy; striated ducts = eosinophilic columnar cells with basal striations.
- Pathweb labels: lobule, connective tissue septa, adipose stroma, excretory (interlobular) duct, intralobular (secretory) ducts = intercalated duct (small, low cuboidal) + striated duct (pink columnar), serous acinus, mucous tubule, serous demilune (serous cap on mucous tubule), blood vessels.
- Gland types: parotid = pure serous + fat; submandibular = mixed, mostly serous; sublingual = mostly mucous.
- Look-alikes: pancreas (islets, no striated ducts); lacrimal; breast. Pathology: Warthin tumour (oncocytic bilayer + lymphoid stroma, smokers), pleomorphic adenoma.

### Seminal vesicle
- ID clues: coiled duct profiles with **elaborate, branching, delicate mucosal folds** lined by simple columnar/pseudostratified epithelium, surrounded by thick smooth muscle.
- Pathweb labels: lumen, mucosal folds with blood vessels, epithelium (simple columnar), smooth muscle.
- Look-alikes: prostate (corpora amylacea, fibromuscular stroma, simpler folds); fallopian tube (ciliated, peg cells, female). Pitfall: seminal vesicle epithelium shows golden lipofuscin and scattered large atypical nuclei that mimic carcinoma (sampled on prostate biopsy).

## Musculoskeletal (continued)

### Skeletal muscle
- ID clues: cross section = polygonal fibres of similar size with **multiple peripheral nuclei**; longitudinal = long unbranched fibres with prominent cross-striations.
- Pathweb labels: muscle fibre, peripheral nucleus, striations (A band dark, I band light).
- Look-alikes: cardiac muscle (central nucleus, branching, intercalated discs); smooth muscle (fusiform cells, central cigar-shaped nucleus, no striations). Pathology: internal nuclei, fibre size variation, necrosis = myopathy; angulated atrophic fibres, fibre-type grouping = neurogenic.

## Skin

### Skin (thin and hair-bearing)
- ID clues: keratinising stratified squamous epidermis with basket-weave stratum corneum over a thick dense-collagen dermis containing adnexa (eccrine coils, hair follicles, sebaceous glands), then subcutaneous fat.
- Pathweb labels: epidermis layers (stratum basale with melanin, spinosum, granulosum with keratohyaline granules, corneum), dermal papillae, dermis (dense irregular connective tissue), subcutis; eccrine sweat gland (pale coiled secretory portion) and duct (darker, more eosinophilic, two-layered cuboidal); pilosebaceous unit = hair bulb with dermal papilla, hair root, cortex, medulla, cuticle, internal and external root sheaths, sebaceous gland (lobules of vacuolated sebocytes), arrector pili smooth muscle.
- Look-alikes: oesophagus / oral mucosa / vagina (non-keratinising, no adnexa); thick skin of palm/sole (very thick compact stratum corneum + stratum lucidum, no hair). Occ-med hooks: allergic and irritant contact dermatitis = spongiosis; arsenic keratosis; tar/pitch → SCC; TEWL relates to stratum corneum barrier.

## Gastrointestinal (continued)

### Small bowel (duodenum, jejunum, ileum)
- ID clues: mucosa with **finger-like villi** over crypts (villus:crypt height ≈ 3–5:1); enterocytes with brush border, goblet cells, Paneth cells at crypt bases.
- Pathweb labels: mucosa, villus, crypt, lamina propria, muscularis mucosae, submucosa, muscularis propria, subserosa; columnar epithelium with brush border, goblet cell, Paneth cell (bright eosinophilic apical granules); duodenum: Brunner glands (pale mucous glands in submucosa, may sit above muscularis mucosae). Ileum: Peyer patches.
- Look-alikes: colon (no villi); stomach (foveolae, no goblet cells normally; intestinal metaplasia if present). Pathology: coeliac disease = villous atrophy, crypt hyperplasia, intraepithelial lymphocytosis (>25/100 enterocytes).

## Nervous system (continued)

### Spinal cord (cervical enlargement)
- ID clues: oval cord section with central H/butterfly-shaped grey matter surrounded by white matter; central canal; ventral median fissure; nerve roots at the dorsolateral sulcus. Cervical enlargement (C5–T1) = wide, oval, with big ventral horns.
- Pathweb labels: dorsal columns (fasciculus gracilis medially = lower limb; fasciculus cuneatus laterally = upper limb; vibration, proprioception, fine touch), dorsal horn, ventral horn (motor neurons), central canal, ventral median fissure, dorsolateral sulcus, nerve root. Luxol fast blue (LFB) stains myelin blue: white matter blue, grey matter pale.
- More labels: ventral horn motor neuron cell bodies (large, polygonal, Nissl-rich; supply upper limb at cervical level), anterior spinal artery in ventral median fissure, central canal lined by ependymal cells (often obliterated/clustered in adults).
- Look-alikes: medulla (olive, pyramids, 4th ventricle); peripheral nerve. Pathology/tox: dorsal column demyelination = B12 deficiency (subacute combined degeneration, also N2O abuse) and tabes dorsalis; anterior horn cell loss = polio/ALS; organophosphate-induced delayed neuropathy affects long tracts.

## Haematolymphoid (continued)

### Spleen
- ID clues: blood-rich red tissue (red pulp) with scattered blue lymphoid islands (white pulp) each centred on an eccentric arteriole; fibrous capsule and trabeculae carrying vessels.
- Pathweb labels: white pulp (periarteriolar lymphoid sheath, lymphoid follicle with germinal centre, central arteriole), red pulp = splenic sinuses (endothelial-lined vascular spaces) + splenic cords (cords of Billroth: reticular cells, macrophages, lymphocytes, RBCs), trabeculae.
- Look-alikes: lymph node (subcapsular sinus, cortex/medulla pattern); haemangioma. Pathology: Gamna-Gandy bodies (siderotic nodules) in congestive splenomegaly; amyloid (sago/lardaceous spleen).

### Stomach (body/corpus and antrum/pylorus)
- ID clues: mucosa = superficial gastric pits (foveolae) lined by tall mucin-capped columnar cells (no goblet cells), with glands below; muscularis mucosae, submucosa, thick 3-layer muscularis propria, serosa.
- Body/fundus (oxyntic): short foveolae (~1/4 of mucosa), long straight glands of **parietal cells** (pink, fried-egg, upper) and **chief cells** (purple-basophilic, deeper); mucous neck cells at the gland neck.
- Antrum/pylorus: longer foveolae (~1/2 of mucosa), coiled pale **mucous (non-specialised) glands** resting on muscularis mucosae; flatter mucosa (fewer rugae); G cells (not seen on H&E).
- Pathweb labels: lumen, gastric pits/foveolae, gastric glands, lamina propria (loose CT, few lymphocytes, vessels), muscularis mucosae, submucosa, muscularis propria, serosa.
- Look-alikes: duodenum (villi, Brunner glands); Brunner glands vs pyloric glands (location). Pathology: H. pylori gastritis (antrum, neutrophils in foveolae), intestinal metaplasia (goblet cells), atrophic gastritis (loss of parietal cells in body).

### Testis and rete testis
- ID clues: closely packed round seminiferous tubules with multilayered germ cells maturing toward the lumen; interstitium with Leydig cells (pink polygonal, Reinke crystals).
- Pathweb labels: seminiferous tubule, lumen, Sertoli cells (pale, triangular nucleus with prominent nucleolus, basal), spermatogonia (basal, small round), primary spermatocytes (large, coarse chromatin), spermatids (small, elongating to dark heads), peritubular myoid cells; rete testis = irregular channels lined by simple columnar/cuboidal epithelium in dense connective tissue (mediastinum testis).
- Look-alikes: atrophic testis (Sertoli-cell-only, thick basement membrane); seminoma (sheets of clear cells + lymphocytes). Occ-med hooks: DBCP, lead, heat → impaired spermatogenesis (maturation arrest, hypospermatogenesis).
- More labels: Leydig (interstitial) cells = clusters of pink polygonal cells between tubules (testosterone); epididymis = tightly coiled duct profiles with smooth round lumen, tall pseudostratified columnar **principal cells with stereocilia**, basal cells, thin smooth muscle coat, sperm in lumen.
- Look-alikes (epididymis): vas deferens (single profile, very thick 3-layer muscle); efferent ductules (scalloped "saw-tooth" lumen of ciliated + non-ciliated cells).

## Haematolymphoid (continued)

### Thymus
- ID clues: lobulated lymphoid organ; each lobule = dark lymphocyte-packed cortex and paler medulla containing **Hassall corpuscles** (concentric whorls of keratinised epithelial cells); in adults largely replaced by fat (involution) with residual islands.
- Pathweb labels: thymic tissue, adipose tissue, thymic cortex, thymic medulla, thymic epithelial cells (pale, vesicular nuclei), lymphocytes (thymocytes), Hassall corpuscle.
- Look-alikes: lymph node (follicles, sinuses, no Hassall bodies); thymoma (lobulated epithelial tumour with lymphocytes, fibrous bands); lymphoblastic lymphoma.

## Endocrine (continued)

### Thyroid
- ID clues: closely packed round follicles of variable size filled with pink colloid, lined by a single layer of cuboidal follicular cells with round nuclei.
- Pathweb labels: follicle, colloid, follicular cell (cuboidal, round nucleus); C cells (parafollicular, calcitonin) not obvious on H&E.
- Look-alikes: kidney cortex (no colloid; thyroidisation of kidney in chronic pyelonephritis mimics it), pars intermedia cysts, struma ovarii. Pathology: papillary carcinoma (ground-glass, grooved overlapping nuclei, pseudoinclusions, psammoma bodies); Hashimoto (lymphoid follicles + Hürthle cells). Occ/env: radiation exposure → papillary carcinoma; iodine deficiency → goitre.

## Head and neck (continued)

### Tongue
- ID clues: stratified squamous mucosa with surface papillae over a core of **interlacing skeletal-muscle bundles running in 3 planes**, with minor salivary gland lobules between muscle.
- Pathweb labels: modified stratified non-keratinising squamous epithelium (keratinised on filiform papillae), papillae, taste buds (pale barrel-shaped clusters in the epithelium, open to the surface via a taste pore; gustatory cells with microvilli), mucous minor salivary gland lobules (posterior: serous von Ebner glands), striated muscle.
- Look-alikes: oesophagus (smooth or mixed muscle in two layers, no taste buds); lip (skin on one side, orbicularis oris); skeletal muscle tumour.
- More labels: mucous minor salivary glands (pale foamy cytoplasmic mucin, flattened basal nuclei), adipose tissue and nerves between muscle bundles.

### Tonsil (palatine)
- ID clues: lymphoid organ covered by non-keratinising stratified squamous epithelium that dives into deep, branching **crypts**; numerous secondary follicles; partial connective-tissue hemicapsule on the deep side only.
- Pathweb labels: epithelium, crypts (with debris), lymphoid nodules/follicles with germinal centre (pale, activated B lymphoblasts) and mantle layer (dark naïve lymphocytes), intraepithelial lymphoid infiltrates (reticulated crypt epithelium can obscure basement membrane), connective tissue hemicapsule/septa, blood vessels. MALT of oro-/nasopharynx.
- Look-alikes: lymph node (capsule all round, subcapsular sinus, no epithelium); adenoid (respiratory epithelium, no deep crypts); lymphoepithelial cyst. Pathology: HPV-related SCC arises in crypt epithelium (p16+).

## Respiratory (continued)

### Trachea
- ID clues: large airway with a **C-shaped hyaline cartilage ring** (open posteriorly, bridged by smooth trachealis muscle); respiratory epithelium; submucosal seromucinous glands.
- Pathweb labels: lumen, cartilage, respiratory epithelium (pseudostratified ciliated columnar with goblet cells), cilia, thick basement membrane, lamina propria, seromucinous glands (darker serous acini + paler mucinous acini), trachealis (smooth muscle).
- Look-alikes: bronchus (cartilage plates, not a ring; smooth muscle layer around); oesophagus (squamous, no cartilage). Occ-med hooks: thickened basement membrane + goblet hyperplasia + eosinophils = asthma (incl. occupational asthma); squamous metaplasia in smokers/irritant exposure.

## Urinary (continued)

### Ureter
- ID clues: small tube with a **stellate (star-shaped) lumen** lined by urothelium, thick muscular wall, adventitia with fat and vessels.
- Pathweb labels: lumen, urothelium (transitional epithelium, multilayered) with umbrella cells, lamina propria, muscularis propria (inner longitudinal, outer circular; extra outer longitudinal in lower third), adventitia.
- Look-alikes: vas deferens (also small lumen + thick muscle, but pseudostratified columnar with stereocilia, 3 muscle layers, in spermatic cord); bladder; urethra.

## Female genital tract (continued)

### Uterus (proliferative endometrium + myometrium)
- ID clues: endometrium of straight-to-coiled tubular glands in a cellular, blue, spindle-cell stroma, abutting thick interlacing smooth-muscle bundles (myometrium).
- Pathweb labels: lumen, endometrium (functionalis/basalis), endometrial gland (columnar epithelium; proliferative = tubular glands with pseudostratified nuclei and mitoses), endometrial stroma, myometrium; endometrial-myometrial junction.
- Cycle dating: proliferative (straight tubular glands, mitoses) → early secretory (subnuclear vacuoles ~day 17) → late secretory (saw-tooth glands, luminal secretion, stromal decidualisation, spiral arterioles) → menstrual (stromal breakdown, blue balls).
- Look-alikes: endometrial hyperplasia (crowded glands, gland:stroma > 1); adenomyosis (endometrium deep in myometrium); low-grade endometrial stromal sarcoma.

## Male genital tract (continued)

### Vas deferens
- ID clues: small, slightly folded lumen inside a **very thick, three-layered smooth-muscle wall** (inner longitudinal, middle circular, outer longitudinal); in the spermatic cord.
- Pathweb labels: pseudostratified columnar epithelium with stereocilia (non-motile, absorptive), inner and outer longitudinal smooth muscle layers, middle circular layer.
- Look-alikes: ureter (urothelium, stellate lumen); epididymis (many duct profiles, thin muscle); artery (endothelium, internal elastic lamina, blood).

## Respiratory (continued)

### Lung
- ID clues: lace-like network of thin alveolar septa enclosing air spaces, with bronchioles and pulmonary vessels travelling together.
- Pathweb labels: alveolus, alveolar septa (type I pneumocytes = flat lining; type II = cuboidal, surfactant; thin-walled capillaries), alveolar macrophages in alveolar spaces, bronchiole (ciliated columnar epithelium, smooth muscle, no cartilage/glands), alveolar duct, blood vessels (pulmonary artery branch beside bronchiole).
- Look-alikes: emphysema (septal destruction, enlarged air spaces); atelectasis (collapsed septa mimic interstitial thickening); vascular lesions.
- **Occupational lung pathology hooks (board):** silicotic nodule = whorled hyalinised collagen with central acellular zone + birefringent particles at polarisation (upper lobes, hilar nodes); coal macule = dust-laden macrophages around respiratory bronchioles + focal emphysema; asbestosis = interstitial fibrosis (lower lobes, subpleural) + ferruginous (asbestos) bodies (golden-brown beaded rods, Perls+); mesothelioma (pleura); berylliosis = non-caseating granulomas mimicking sarcoid; hard metal (cobalt) = giant-cell interstitial pneumonia; hypersensitivity pneumonitis = bronchiolocentric poorly formed granulomas.

## Quick-reference: confusable pairs

| Pair | Separating feature |
|---|---|
| Cardiac vs skeletal muscle | central 1–2 nuclei, branching, intercalated discs vs peripheral multiple nuclei, unbranched |
| Skeletal vs smooth muscle | striations + peripheral nuclei vs fusiform cells, central cigar nucleus |
| Colon vs small bowel | no villi vs villi + Paneth cells |
| Appendix vs colon | circumferential lymphoid tissue, small lumen |
| Stomach body vs antrum | parietal/chief cells, short pits vs mucous glands, long pits |
| Duodenum vs jejunum/ileum | Brunner glands vs none / Peyer patches (ileum) |
| Oesophagus vs skin vs vagina/ectocervix | submucosal glands + muscularis mucosae vs keratin + adnexa vs glycogenated, no muscularis mucosae |
| Gallbladder vs bile duct | loose muscle coat, no submucosa vs dense collagen wall + peribiliary glands |
| Pancreas vs parotid vs lacrimal | islets + centroacinar cells vs striated ducts + fat vs pure serous lobules, no striated ducts |
| Salivary vs breast | serous zymogen granules, striated ducts vs TDLUs with myoepithelial layer |
| Thyroid vs kidney cortex vs pars intermedia | colloid in many follicles vs glomeruli/tubules vs a few cysts between ant./post. pituitary |
| Parathyroid vs thyroid | sheets of chief cells + fat vs colloid follicles |
| Adrenal vs liver vs clear-cell RCC | zonation, no portal tracts vs plates + portal tracts vs nests with delicate vessels, atypia |
| Lymph node vs tonsil vs spleen vs thymus | subcapsular sinus vs surface squamous + crypts vs red pulp + arteriole-centred white pulp vs Hassall corpuscles |
| Trachea vs bronchus vs bronchiole | C-ring + trachealis vs cartilage plates + glands vs no cartilage, no glands |
| Ureter vs vas deferens | urothelium, stellate lumen vs stereocilia, 3 thick muscle layers |
| Prostate vs seminal vesicle | corpora amylacea, fibromuscular stroma vs elaborate folds, lipofuscin, atypical nuclei |
| Fallopian tube vs seminal vesicle | ciliated + peg cells vs non-ciliated, smooth muscle coat |
| Epididymis vs vas deferens | many coiled profiles, thin muscle vs single lumen, thick muscle |
| Cerebrum vs cerebellum vs hippocampus | 6-layer cortex vs molecular/Purkinje/granular vs interlocking C-bands of CA + dentate |
| Midbrain vs pons vs medulla | aqueduct + substantia nigra vs bulky basal pons + 4th ventricle vs olive + pyramids |
| Cornea vs sclera vs conjunctiva | regular avascular lamellae vs irregular dense collagen vs goblet cells + vessels |
| Compact vs cancellous vs woven bone | osteons vs trabeculae + marrow vs haphazard collagen, plump osteocytes |
